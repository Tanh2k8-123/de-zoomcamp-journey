# Module 7 Unit 6 — Why Flink?

## Core ideas

- Flink supplies stateful processing, event-time windows, parallelism, and checkpoint coordination.
- A plain Python consumer remains useful for simple processing, but sophisticated state/recovery needs more machinery.
- The choice depends on latency, volume, correctness needs, and operational complexity.
